The Story

One owner. One crew. Fifty years of backyards.

Don Gentili started cleaning and repairing pools in Huntington Beach in 1975. By 1987 he was building them — and he found his calling in the exotic ones: rock lagoons, beach entries, and grotto waterfalls that give the illusion it isn't really a pool, just a private watering hole you happened to stumble across.

Every rock is cast from the real thing and finished with hand-carved detail. And because Don deliberately keeps the company small, the man who designs your pool is on site while it's built — and still answers the phone years after it's done.

“We turn average backyards into tomorrow's vacation land.” Don Gentili · Owner
